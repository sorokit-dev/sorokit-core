# Code of Conduct

We want sorokit-dev to be a welcoming place to build, ask questions, and contribute, whatever your background or experience level.

## Our standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept feedback graciously. Reviews are about the work.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Credit others' work, and only submit work you have the right to submit.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, including low-effort or bad-faith pull requests made only to collect rewards or points.
- Trying to manipulate contribution programs, for example through multiple accounts.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block contributors who break these standards. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately using the contact details on the [sorokit-dev organization profile](https://github.com/sorokit-dev). Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted in spirit from widely used open-source codes of conduct, including the Contributor Covenant.
